Eggscape Privacy Policy

Arisesai Games · Last updated October 23, 2026

This policy explains what information the Eggscape game ("the app") uses, made by Arisesai Games ("we", "us"). The app does not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location.

Information saved on your device

Your best height, coins, power-ups, daily reward progress, sound setting, and whether you bought "Remove ads" are saved only on your device. We do not receive this information. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a daily reminder on your device about your daily treasure. It is created on your phone; we do not send or receive any data for it. You can turn notifications off in Android settings.

Advertising

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. To show, limit, and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • device information (model, operating system, language)
  • how you interact with ads

Where the law requires it, including in the EEA, the UK and some US states, the app asks for your choice before personalized ads are shown. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android settings: Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads on some phones).

Purchases

The "Remove ads" purchase and coin packs are processed by Google Play. We do not see or store your payment details. Google Play tells the app only whether the purchase was made, so ads can be turned off.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising and purchase services described above, the app does not share data with anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
